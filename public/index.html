Frequently asked questions

What devices does Garden Pro Planner support?

Garden Pro Planner is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store, and runs on iOS 15 and later.

How does the planting calendar know my dates?

The calendar is tuned to your location's frost dates. Once you set your location, the app calculates when to start seeds indoors, transplant outdoors, and expect to harvest each plant.

Can I sync planting and harvest events to my calendar?

Yes. Planting and harvest events can sync to your iOS and Google Calendar, with alerts and watering reminders so nothing gets missed.

How big is the plant library?

The library includes 225+ plants, each with details on sunlight, soil, pH, watering needs, expected yield, pests, and whether it's safe around pets.

What languages are supported?

Nine: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese.
