Support

Answers to the most common questions about Garden Pro Planner.

Getting started

Create your first garden, add one or more areas, and set each area’s sunlight, soil type, and pH. Then browse the 225+ plant library and add plants that match your conditions. Set your location so the planting calendar can tune itself to your local frost dates.

Setting your location and frost dates

Open Settings and set your location. The app uses your location’s frost dates to calculate when to start seeds indoors, transplant outdoors, and expect to harvest. You can update your location any time and the calendar recalculates automatically.

Syncing planting & harvest events to your calendar

Planting and harvest events can sync to your iOS and Google Calendar with alerts. Enable calendar sync and grant calendar access when prompted. You can also turn on watering reminders so nothing gets missed.

Finding the right plants

Use advanced filtering in the plant library to narrow by category, sunlight, soil, pH, watering needs, and pet safety, so you only see plants suited to a given area.

Tracking yield & savings

Each plant shows an expected yield based on how many you grow. Log what you harvest and the app adds up your estimated seasonal savings and keeps a running harvest log.

Restoring purchases

If you upgraded and don’t see your purchase on a new device, open Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Managing your data

Your gardens and logs are stored on your device and, if enabled, synced to your private account. You can delete individual gardens, plans, or your data from within the app at any time. See the privacy policy for details.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all of its data from Settings → Delete account inside the app. See Delete your account for the full steps, what is removed, and how to request deletion by email if you have already uninstalled the app.

Languages & devices

Garden Pro Planner runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS 15+) and is available in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese. The app follows your device language automatically.
