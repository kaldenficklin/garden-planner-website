Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Garden Pro Planner (“the app”) is built and operated by Kalden Ficklin. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Information stored on your device

The gardens, plans, plant selections, harvest logs, location and frost-date settings, reminders, and preferences you create are stored on your device and, if you enable sync, in your private account so your data is available across your devices. This information is used only to provide the features of the app. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

The app uses trusted third-party services to operate, sync data, and understand and improve reliability. These may collect limited technical information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — account sync, authentication, and cloud storage of your data
  • Google Analytics for Firebase & Crashlytics — anonymous usage and crash reporting
  • Apple — App Store delivery and, if you make a purchase, in-app purchase processing

Log & diagnostic data

If the app encounters an error, diagnostic information may be collected through the services above. This can include device type, operating system version, app configuration at the time, and the date and time of the event. It is used to diagnose problems and improve the app.

Purchases

Any purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Data security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can delete your gardens and data at any time from within the app. Removing the app from your device removes the data stored locally on it.

To permanently delete your account and everything stored with it, see Delete your account, which explains exactly what is removed, what is kept, and how to request deletion by email.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
