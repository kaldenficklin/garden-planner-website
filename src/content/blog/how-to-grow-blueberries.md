Acid soil, two varieties for pollination, and the shallow roots that make watering and mulch matter more than feeding.

## The steps

1. **Give Them Acid Soil.** They need acid conditions. Ordinary soil turns leaves yellow.
2. **Use Ericaceous Compost.** Best grown in a large pot. Easier than changing a bed.
3. **Plant Two Varieties.** Two types crop far better. Pick ones that overlap.
4. **Full Sun.** Sun makes sweeter fruit. Some shade is tolerated.
5. **Water with Rainwater.** Tap water raises the pH. Collect it if you can.
6. **Mulch the Surface.** Roots sit very shallow. Mulch keeps them damp.
7. **Feed Ericaceous Only.** Ordinary feed harms them. Feed in spring only.
8. **Prune Old Wood.** Best fruit on young stems. Cut the oldest at the base.


**Pro tip.** Yellow leaves with green veins almost always mean the soil is not acid enough rather than short of feed. Adding fertiliser at that point makes it worse, not better.
