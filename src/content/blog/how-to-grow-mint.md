Keeping mint contained, keeping it tender, and the one thing to do the day you bring it home.

## The steps

1. **Keep It Potted.** It spreads by underground runners. A bed is lost in one season.
2. **Part Shade Is Fine.** Full sun is not required. Shade keeps leaves tender.
3. **Keep Soil Damp.** Mint wilts fast when dry. It recovers if watered soon.
4. **Pinch Often.** Pinch tips to keep it bushy. Stops it going leggy.
5. **Remove Flowers.** Flowering thins the flavour. Pinch buds as they form.
6. **Cut It Back Hard.** Cut low when it gets woody. It regrows from the base.
7. **Divide Every Two Years.** Pots exhaust themselves. Split and repot in spring.
8. **Harvest from the Top.** Take the top few leaves. Never strip a whole stem.


**Pro tip.** If you want mint in a bed, sink the whole pot into the soil with the rim standing proud. You get the look of a planted clump and the roots still cannot escape sideways.
