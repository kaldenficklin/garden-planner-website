Container space is limited, and a slow crop can tie up a pot for months before you see anything for it. If you're planning a balcony or patio garden and want to actually harvest something before the season turns, growing speed is worth planning around as much as sun and soil.

The chart below runs fastest to slowest, from microgreens ready inside two weeks to peppers that want a full summer of warmth. Somewhere in the middle tends to be the sweet spot — fast enough to feel like progress, productive enough to be worth the pot it's sitting in.
