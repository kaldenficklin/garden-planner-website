Eight steps to a productive tomato plant, from picking a variety through to knowing when the fruit is actually ready.

## The steps

1. **Pick a Variety.** Determinate: one big harvest. Indeterminate: fruits all season.
2. **Give Full Sun.** Six to eight hours daily. More sun, more fruit.
3. **Plant Deep.** Bury two thirds of the stem. Buried stem grows new roots.
4. **Water Steadily.** Keep the soil evenly moist. Water the base, not leaves.
5. **Feed Regularly.** Compost at planting time. Low-nitrogen feed once flowering.
6. **Support It.** Cage, stake or trellis. Add support on planting day.
7. **Prune Suckers.** Pinch suckers on indeterminates. Strip low leaves for airflow.
8. **Harvest.** Pick at full colour. Slightly soft to the touch.


**Pro tip.** Mulch two to three inches around each plant. It holds moisture, blocks weeds, and stops the soil splash that spreads disease onto the lower leaves.
