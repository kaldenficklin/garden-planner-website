Eight steps to steady salad leaves, and the heat and timing mistakes that turn a crop bitter overnight.

## The steps

1. **Sow in Cool Weather.** Spring and autumn are best. Heat turns leaves bitter.
2. **Sow Shallow.** Barely cover the seed. Light helps it germinate.
3. **Give Light Shade.** Afternoon shade in summer. Shade delays bolting.
4. **Keep Soil Damp.** Shallow roots dry out fast. Water little and often.
5. **Thin Early.** Thin to a hand's width. Crowding gives small heads.
6. **Feed Lightly.** Compost is usually enough. Leafy crops want nitrogen.
7. **Cut, Don't Pull.** Take outer leaves only. The centre keeps growing.
8. **Pull at Bolting.** A tall stem means bitter. Pull it and sow again.


**Pro tip.** Sow a short row every two weeks rather than one long row at once. Lettuce goes from perfect to bitter in a matter of days, so a steady trickle beats a glut you cannot eat in time.
