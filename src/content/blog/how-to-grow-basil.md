Eight steps to a bushy basil plant, and the pinching habit that keeps it producing instead of running to seed.

## The steps

1. **Wait for Warmth.** Basil hates cold soil. Plant well after last frost.
2. **Full Sun.** Six to eight hours daily. Leggy growth means too little.
3. **Free-Draining Soil.** Basil rots in soggy ground. Pots need drainage holes.
4. **Water the Soil.** Water when the top feels dry. Keep the leaves dry.
5. **Pinch the Tips.** Cut above a pair of leaves. One stem becomes two.
6. **Remove Flower Buds.** Flowering turns leaves bitter. Pinch buds the day you see them.
7. **Feed Lightly.** Too much feed thins the flavour. A little compost is plenty.
8. **Harvest from the Top.** Take the top, not the base. Never strip more than a third.


**Pro tip.** Pinch the growing tip once the plant has two or three sets of true leaves, and keep pinching every couple of weeks. Each cut turns one stem into two, and a pinched plant stays productive far longer.
