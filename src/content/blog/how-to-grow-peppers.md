Warmth, feeding and the flower-drop problem, in eight steps for sweet peppers and chillies alike.

## The steps

1. **Start Early Indoors.** Sow eight weeks before planting. They need a long season.
2. **Wait for Warm Soil.** Cold soil stalls them. Plant well after frost.
3. **Full Sun.** Six to eight hours daily. Sun drives the fruit.
4. **Pinch First Flowers.** Remove the earliest blooms. The plant builds a frame.
5. **Feed Low Nitrogen.** High nitrogen gives all leaf. Switch once flowers open.
6. **Water Steadily.** Dry spells drop the flowers. Never let it wilt.
7. **Support Heavy Plants.** Stake once fruit sets. Loaded branches snap.
8. **Pick to Keep Picking.** Green picks give more fruit. Leave them on to ripen red.


**Pro tip.** Peppers drop their flowers when nights stay hot or the plant is thirsty. Steady water and a little afternoon shade in a heatwave will save a crop that otherwise sets nothing.
