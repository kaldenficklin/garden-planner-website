The window for a fall harvest doesn't close all at once, it closes crop by crop, and it's easy to lose track of which ones you've already missed. The chart above ranks five reliable direct-sow options from fastest to slowest, so you can hold it up against how many weeks you actually have left before frost.

Radishes are the forgiving one, worth a try almost any time this list is still relevant. Bush beans are the gamble, only worth the bed space if your frost date is genuinely weeks out rather than days.
