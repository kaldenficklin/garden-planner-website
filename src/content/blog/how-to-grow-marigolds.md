Sowing, spacing and the deadheading habit that turns a few weeks of flowers into a whole season.

## The steps

1. **Sow After Frost.** They will not take cold. Sow direct or start indoors.
2. **Full Sun.** Six or more hours daily. Shade means few flowers.
3. **Poor Soil Is Fine.** Rich soil gives leaf, not bloom. Skip the fertiliser.
4. **Do Not Overwater.** Let the surface dry. Soggy roots rot.
5. **Space for Airflow.** Crowding brings mildew. Give each plant room.
6. **Deadhead Often.** Snip spent blooms every few days. Seed setting stops flowering.
7. **Plant Among Veg.** They edge a bed well. Popular with beneficial insects.
8. **Save the Seed.** Let a few heads dry. Free plants for next year.


**Pro tip.** Rich soil and heavy feeding give you a big leafy marigold with few flowers. They bloom hardest in poor ground, which makes them ideal for the awkward corners of a vegetable bed.
