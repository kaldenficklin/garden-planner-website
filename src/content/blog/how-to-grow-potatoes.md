Chitting, planting depth, and why mounding soil up the stems is what actually decides your yield.

## The steps

1. **Use Seed Potatoes.** Buy certified seed potatoes. Shop ones may carry disease.
2. **Chit Them First.** Sprout them in a light place. Short green shoots, not long.
3. **After the Frost.** Frost kills new shoots. Plant when soil warms.
4. **Plant Deep.** About a hand's depth. Eyes facing upward.
5. **Hill as They Grow.** Mound soil up the stems. More stem, more tubers.
6. **Keep Them Covered.** Light turns tubers green. Green potatoes are toxic.
7. **Water Steadily.** Most needed while flowering. Erratic water splits tubers.
8. **Harvest by Type.** New potatoes after flowering. Maincrop once tops die back.


**Pro tip.** Tubers form along the buried stem, not below the roots. Every time you mound soil up a growing plant you add more stem for potatoes to form on, which is why hilling matters more than feeding.
