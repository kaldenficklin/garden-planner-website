Why carrots fork, why they never come up, and the thinning most gardeners skip and then regret.

## The steps

1. **Loosen Deep Soil.** Dig a foot down, stone free. Stones and lumps fork roots.
2. **Skip Fresh Manure.** Rich manure splits roots. Use a bed composted last year.
3. **Sow Direct.** Carrots hate transplanting. Sow where they will grow.
4. **Sow Shallow.** Barely cover the seed. Too deep and it never surfaces.
5. **Keep Soil Damp.** Never let the row dry out. Dry seed simply will not come up.
6. **Thin Ruthlessly.** Thin to two fingers apart. Crowding gives stubby roots.
7. **Thin in the Evening.** Bruised leaves attract carrot fly. Water after thinning.
8. **Harvest Young.** Pull when shoulders show. Sweeter before they go woody.


**Pro tip.** Carrot seed is tiny and needs constant moisture to germinate, which is the usual reason a row never comes up. Cover the row with a board or damp cloth and check daily, lifting it the moment green shows.
