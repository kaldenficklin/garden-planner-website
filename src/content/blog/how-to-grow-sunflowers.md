Sowing depth, spacing, staking and when to cut, for sunflowers that stand up through a summer storm.

## The steps

1. **Sow Direct.** They resent root disturbance. Direct sowing beats transplanting.
2. **Sow After Frost.** Wait for warm soil. Cold ground rots the seed.
3. **Plant an Inch Deep.** About an inch down. Firm the soil over them.
4. **Give Them Room.** Giants need a foot or more. Crowding means small heads.
5. **Full Sun, All Day.** Six to eight hours minimum. They lean hard toward light.
6. **Water Deeply.** Soak the root zone weekly. Deep roots resist wind.
7. **Stake Tall Types.** Stake before the head fills. A heavy head snaps in wind.
8. **Cut or Leave.** Cut as petals start lifting. Or leave the heads for birds.


**Pro tip.** Sow a short row every two weeks from spring through midsummer instead of all at once. You get sunflowers in bloom for months rather than one spectacular fortnight and then bare stalks.
