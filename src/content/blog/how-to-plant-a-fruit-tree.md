Hole width, graft height, watering and the two mistakes that kill more young fruit trees than anything else.

## The steps

1. **Check Pollination.** Many need a second variety. Check before you buy one.
2. **Plant When Dormant.** Late autumn through early spring. Roots settle before growth starts.
3. **Dig Wide, Not Deep.** Three times the root width. Same depth as the pot.
4. **Keep the Graft High.** Graft bump above soil level. Buried grafts lose the dwarfing.
5. **Skip the Fertiliser.** No feed in the planting hole. Roots must reach out.
6. **Backfill and Firm.** Use the soil you dug out. Firm gently, leave no gaps.
7. **Mulch, Not Touching.** Ring of mulch around it. Keep it off the trunk.
8. **Water Two Years.** Deep soak weekly when dry. Drought kills more than cold.


**Pro tip.** Most young fruit trees are lost in their first two summers, and almost always to drought rather than cold. One long slow soak a week through dry spells beats a daily sprinkle every time.
