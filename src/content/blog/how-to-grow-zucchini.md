Spacing, pollination and picking size, plus the male-and-female flower problem behind most fruitless plants.

## The steps

1. **Give It Space.** One plant fills a square metre. Crowding brings mildew.
2. **Wait for Warmth.** Cold soil rots the seed. Sow after the last frost.
3. **Rich Soil.** Dig in plenty of compost. They are hungry plants.
4. **Water at the Base.** Wet leaves grow mildew. Water in the morning.
5. **Know the Flowers.** Male flowers sit on stems. Female ones have a tiny fruit.
6. **Hand-Pollinate.** Brush pollen into the female. Do it in the morning.
7. **Pick Small.** Best at finger to hand length. Marrows stop the plant.
8. **Pick Every Other Day.** Missed fruit slows production. Keep the plant cropping.


**Pro tip.** Early flowers are nearly all male, so nothing sets and it looks like failure. Wait a week or two for the female flowers, the ones with a tiny fruit behind the bloom, before worrying.
