Which type to plant, why beans hate transplanting, and the picking habit that keeps a plant cropping for weeks.

## The steps

1. **Bush or Pole.** Bush: quick, all at once. Pole: slower, crops for weeks.
2. **Sow Direct.** Beans resent transplanting. Sow where they will grow.
3. **Wait for Warm Soil.** Cold soil rots the seed. Sow after the last frost.
4. **Skip the Nitrogen.** Beans make their own. Nitrogen gives leaf, not pods.
5. **Support Pole Types.** Put the frame up first. Roots hate later digging.
6. **Water at Flowering.** Dry spells drop the flowers. Steady water while podding.
7. **Pick Young.** Pick before seeds bulge. Older pods turn stringy.
8. **Pick Every Other Day.** Left pods stop the plant. Regular picking keeps it going.


**Pro tip.** Beans fix their own nitrogen, so a rich nitrogen feed gives you a huge leafy plant and almost no pods. Compost at planting is all they need.
