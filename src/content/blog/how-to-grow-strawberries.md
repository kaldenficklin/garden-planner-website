What strawberry plants actually need, from crown depth at planting through to keeping the fruit off wet soil.

## The steps

1. **Pick a Type.** June-bearing: one heavy crop. Everbearing: smaller, spread out.
2. **Full Sun.** Six or more hours daily. Shade means small, sour fruit.
3. **Set the Crown Right.** Crown level with the soil. Buried crowns rot, exposed dry.
4. **Space Them Out.** About a foot apart. Crowding invites grey mould.
5. **Water at the Base.** Steady moisture while fruiting. Wet leaves spread disease.
6. **Mulch Underneath.** Straw keeps fruit off soil. Cleaner berries, less rot.
7. **Manage Runners.** Cut runners for bigger fruit. Or root a few for new plants.
8. **Pick Fully Red.** They do not ripen after picking. Pick every day or two.


**Pro tip.** Pinch off the first flush of flowers on a new plant. You lose a few early berries and get a far stronger plant, and a much bigger crop, for the rest of the season.
