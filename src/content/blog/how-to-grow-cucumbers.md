Growing them vertically, keeping the water steady, and the stress that makes a cucumber turn bitter.

## The steps

1. **Grow Them Up.** A trellis saves bed space. Fruit stays clean and straight.
2. **Wait for Warm Soil.** They stall in the cold. Sow after the last frost.
3. **Full Sun.** Six or more hours daily. Sun drives the growth.
4. **Rich, Draining Soil.** Dig in plenty of compost. They are hungry and thirsty.
5. **Water Steadily.** Uneven water turns them bitter. Never let the plant wilt.
6. **Mulch the Roots.** Keeps moisture even. Also keeps the soil cooler.
7. **Water the Soil.** Wet leaves get mildew. Water in the morning.
8. **Pick Young and Often.** Small ones taste best. Big ones stop the plant.


**Pro tip.** Bitterness comes from stress, usually uneven watering or heat. A cucumber that dried out once will taste bitter no matter what you do afterwards, so consistency beats rescue.
